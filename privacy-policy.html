Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Rangrez ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you shop with us, how we use it, and how it is protected.

Information we collect

  • Name, phone number, and email address (if provided)
  • Delivery address, city, state and pincode
  • Order details — items purchased, quantities, and order value
  • Payment status (we do not store your card, UPI or bank details — these are handled directly and securely by our payment partner, Razorpay)

How we use your information

  • To process, confirm and deliver your order
  • To contact you about your order via WhatsApp, phone, or email
  • To send an order confirmation or cancellation notice
  • To improve our products and shopping experience

Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone. Your details are shared only with the service providers needed to fulfil your order — our payment gateway (Razorpay) for processing payments, and courier partners for delivery.

Cookies and local storage

Our website uses your browser's local storage to remember your shopping cart and recent orders on your own device, so you don't lose your cart if you refresh the page. This information stays on your device and is not shared.

Data security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information. Online payments are processed through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway — we never see or store your full card or bank details.

Your rights

You can ask us to review, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us (see Contact Us).
