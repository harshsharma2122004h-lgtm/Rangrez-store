Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Cancelling an order

You can cancel your order yourself, any time before it is dispatched, using the "Track / Cancel Order" option on our website (you'll need your phone number and the Order ID you were given at checkout).

Once cancelled: if you paid Cash on Delivery, no charge applies. If you paid online, we will confirm your refund with you on WhatsApp and process it as described below.

Refunds for online payments

  • Approved refunds are processed to your original payment method via Razorpay.
  • Refunds typically reflect in your account within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.
  • You will be notified on WhatsApp/email once a refund has been initiated.

Returns after delivery

  • If you receive a damaged, defective, or wrong item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the product and packaging.
  • Since our fabric, kurtis and shirts are hand block-printed and cut to order, we are unable to accept returns for reasons of personal preference (e.g. change of mind about colour/print) unless the item is genuinely defective or incorrect.
  • Approved returns will be replaced or refunded after the item is received back by us in its original condition.

Non-cancellable / non-returnable items

Custom or made-to-order pieces, and items marked "Final Sale" (if any), cannot be cancelled once production/dispatch has started, except in case of a defect on our part.

To cancel, return, or ask about a refund, use Track / Cancel Order on the site, or contact us — see Contact Us.
