Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Shipping charges

  • Free shipping on all orders above ₹700.
  • Orders below ₹700, delivered within Rajasthan: ₹99
  • Orders below ₹700, delivered outside Rajasthan: ₹149

The exact shipping charge for your order is calculated automatically at checkout based on your delivery state, and shown in the order summary before you pay.

Delivery timelines

  • Orders are typically dispatched within 1–3 business days of confirmation.
  • Delivery generally takes 3–7 business days within Rajasthan and 5–10 business days for the rest of India, depending on your location and courier serviceability.
  • These are estimates, not guarantees — actual delivery time can vary due to courier delays, weather, or remote locations.

Order tracking

We'll share delivery updates with you on WhatsApp/phone once your order is dispatched. You can also check your order status any time using Track / Cancel Order on our website.

Address accuracy

Please double-check your address, pincode and phone number at checkout — we are not responsible for delays or non-delivery caused by incorrect address details.
